\begin{frame}{One CWL workflow: pgr-tk extraction, Immuannot typing, pgr-tk and pggb graphs}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item 610 haplotypes: APR (UAE Arab) 106, HPRC release 2 464, JaSaPaGe Saudi 18 and Japanese 20, GRCh38, CHM13
    \item MHC (GRCh38 chr6:28.5--33.5 Mb, 100 kb flank) pulled from each assembly with pgr-tk \texttt{pgr-query}, about 30 s per genome
    \item Immuannot (IPD-IMGT/HLA 3.55) gives gene structure and full-resolution allele calls per haplotype
    \item Per gene (19 genes): sequences cut at the Immuannot coordinates, pgr-tk principal-bundle plots, pggb graphs rendered with odgi
    \item Whole MHC and the class II block (DRA to DMA): pgr-tk bundle decomposition, dendrograms, bundle graphs
    \item Runs on the NIG node without containers (Toil, \texttt{--no-container}); repo \texttt{github.com/leechuck/pangenome-bh26}
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{How: MHC extraction with pgr-tk (workflow steps prepare\_reference, extract\_pgrtk)}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Query: GRCh38 chr6:28,510,120--33,480,577 plus 100 kb flank, cut from the PanSN GRCh38 fasta with samtools faidx
    \item Per assembly: \texttt{pgr-query --fastx-file -w 80 -k 56 -r 4 --min-span 64 --merge-range-tol 100000} (SHIMMER index of the whole assembly in memory, 8 threads, about 30 s and 9 GB per 3 Gb haplotype)
    \item Hits under 20 kb or under 20 anchors dropped; minus-strand hits reverse-complemented; records renamed \texttt{sample\#hap\#contig:start-end}; coverage = query span covered by kept hits (\texttt{scripts/pgr\_query\_rename.py})
    \item Assemblies already PanSN-named (HPRC r2, references, JaSaPaGe NA*) are not prefixed twice; samples present in two projects get a \texttt{.cohort} suffix
    \item Concordance check: full-resolution Immuannot calls of the five JPT individuals compared between their HPRC and JaSaPaGe assemblies, per gene, as unordered genotypes
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{How: allele calling and aggregation (steps immuannot, aggregate)}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Immuannot v3 (\texttt{immuannot.sh -c <mhc.fa> -r Data-2024Feb02 --diff 0.03}, 4 threads) on each extracted MHC: IPD-IMGT/HLA 3.55 gen sequences, IPD-KIR 2.13, RefSeq C4; 610 runs, about 2 h on 28 cores
    \item Allele = Immuannot \texttt{consensus} field of the transcript row; \texttt{:new} marks a full-length sequence absent from the database; novel coding vs noncoding from \texttt{cds\_distance}
    \item \texttt{scripts/aggregate\_immuannot.py}: hla\_calls.tsv (one row per gene copy), haplotype x gene matrix, copy numbers (C4A/B long/short, DRB3/4/5 presence), per-cohort two-field frequencies and plots
    \item Cohorts: assembly folder plus file-name rule; JaSaPaGe split into Saudi (ksa*) and Japanese (NA*, 1000G JPT) in \texttt{scripts/make\_inputs.py}
    \item Figure: \texttt{analysis/fig5\_population.py}
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{How: novel-allele support (scripts/novel\_alleles.py, analysis/fig4)}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Immuannot \texttt{cds\_mut} gives, per transcript, the closest allele and the CDS-relative differences (cs-style: N matching bases then a substitution, assembly base first); converted to contig coordinates through the CDS rows, strand-aware
    \item Independent-assembly support: the 31-mer centred on each variant (and its reverse complement) searched in the same gene cut from all other 609 haplotypes; count of carriers, and whether the other haplotype of the same individual carries it
    \item Read support (HPRC individuals only): 1000G Illumina reads of the same person realigned to the assembled contig (minimap2 -ax sr), samtools mpileup $\pm$40 bp around the novel codon, per-base counts
    \item Indel-only ``new'' alleles (ksa004 HLA-A, HG03516 DPA1) flagged as likely HiFi homopolymer errors; APR raw reads are not public (SharePoint holds only DeepVariant VCFs)
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{How: class II bundle decomposition with pgr-tk (scripts/extract\_span.py, tools/pgr\_pbundle.cwl, analysis/classII\_bundles.py); also slides 10 and 11}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Span: from HLA-DRA to HLA-DMA (outermost Immuannot coordinates, 20 kb flank) cut from every MHC haplotype in DRA orientation with samtools faidx; 610 sequences, 461--650 kb
    \item \texttt{pgr-pbundle-decomp -w 48 -k 56 -r 2 --min-span 8 --min-branch-size 8 --bundle-length-cutoff 500 --bundle-merge-distance 2000}: 863 principal bundles; \texttt{pgr-pbundle-bed2dist} for the distance matrix and dendrogram, \texttt{bed2sorted} for the bundle-presence vectors, \texttt{bed2svg} for the bundle plot (classII.svg/html)
    \item PCA (this slide): 608 x 863 bundle presence matrix, centred, SVD; colour = secondary DRB gene from Immuannot
    \item Dendrogram (slide 10): Newick from bed2dist, leaves mapped to haplotypes, colour strips from the allele calls; cluster purity at 10--80 clusters in \texttt{classII\_cluster\_purity.tsv}
    \item Bundle graph (slide 11): nodes = bundles from the bed file, edges = consecutive bundles along each haplotype weighted by haplotype count, Kamada-Kawai layout
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{How: per-gene sequence sets and graphs (steps gene\_extract, gene\_bundle, gene\_graph, gene\_viz, gene\_draw)}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Gene sequences: every annotated copy cut at its Immuannot coordinates with 2 kb flank, gene orientation, named \texttt{sample\#hap\#GENE} (\texttt{scripts/extract\_genes.py}); a pgr-query homology fetch is available as alternative but needs denser seeds (w=24/k=32; DRB1 w=16/k=24) than the defaults
    \item pgr-tk per gene: \texttt{pgr-pbundle-decomp -w 48 -k 56 -r 2 --bundle-length-cutoff 200 --bundle-merge-distance 1000}, then bed2dist / bed2sorted / bed2svg
    \item pggb 0.7.4 per gene: \texttt{-p 90 -s 2000 -n <sequences> -P asm20}; odgi viz (1D, rows = haplotypes) and odgi layout + draw (2D)
    \item Whole MHC: pgr-pbundle-decomp w=80, k=56, r=6, min\_span 28 on the 3 Gb MHC fasta (about 40 min); optional whole-MHC pggb graph not run
    \item Runtime: Toil single-machine on 28 cores; extraction, annotation and graphs about 4 h in total
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}


\begin{frame}{Take-home}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Assembly-based HLA typing across three pangenome projects reproduces known population frequencies and reveals data problems (duplicated haplotype, homozygous individuals) that short reads alone would not
    \item 60--78\% of DRB1 gene copies have no full-length match in IPD-IMGT/HLA; Arab haplotypes (APR + Saudi, 124) are a submission-ready resource once read support is added
    \item pgr-tk bundle decomposition recovers DR haplogroup structure without any allele calls; scales from 94 to 610 haplotypes
    \item Open: APR and JaSaPaGe raw reads for novel-allele validation; KIR and IGH with the same workflow; HPRC per-population split
    \item Workflow, scripts, figures and tables: \texttt{github.com/leechuck/pangenome-bh26} (\texttt{hla/})
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
